Super Admin Overview · D: A's cohort map + B's written briefing

A calm, authoritative department overview, written as a short briefing. One headline sentence, then three numbered chapters, one for each approved DEC-281 analytic: distribution by section (the cohort map, in academic order), category share and resolved vs unresolved. Each has a written takeaway and a path into the records. No other analytics, KPI tiles or counts. Sample data is fictional.

Focal point

  • The masthead sentence ("33 complaints are still unresolved."), closed by an ink rule, and then the cohort map, the largest visual on the page.

01 · Distribution by section

  • Rows in academic order (Semester 1 → 6) with the batch beneath; columns are sections. Each cell shows its unresolved count and its total, so shading is read in context.
  • Shading is mixed from ink and surface, never red, and never sorted into a ranking.
  • Every cell is a link into that exact cohort's records (DEC-205).

02 · Category share

  • One proportion bar plus a list in the configured category order (DEC-220), with counts and percentages, and a link into complaints by category.

03 · Resolved vs unresolved

  • Two large numerals over a split rule. Resolved uses the Resolved status tone, because it is status; unresolved stays neutral.
  • A visible footnote on how Rejected complaints are counted.

Paths into records

  • Map cells, the category link, the unresolved link, and a "Go to" row (no numbers) for complaints, collective issues, calendar, students and Batch Advisors.

Mobile (360px)

  • The masthead, then the chapters stacked. The map keeps its three section columns, with batch names dropped from row labels; the takeaway sentence names the cohort in full.

Trade-offs

  • A briefing is read top to bottom; it is less of a "control panel". That is deliberate: control lives in Complaints and Statistics.
  • Showing totals ("of 19") alongside counts adds numbers, but they belong to the same distribution analytic.

Open questions

  • How Rejected complaints count in resolved vs unresolved.
  • Time window: current semester or all time.
  • Whether each batch is in exactly one semester at a time (the map assumes so).
  • Small-section privacy (O25).
