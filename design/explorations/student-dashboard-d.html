Phone · 360px (scroll inside)

Focal point

  • The welcome: a display-serif greeting and a calculated personal summary that says what changed ("Your Batch Advisor replied…") and what is waiting. Nothing else on the page is set this large.
  • When nothing has changed, the summary says so plainly, for example: "Nothing needs your attention today."

Your actions

  • Journey card only when there is news. It appears for a complaint with new activity: a reply, a status change or a resolution. Otherwise Your actions is a quiet list.
  • The card pairs the journey (left) with the actual reply (right), so the student reads the news without opening the case.
  • Other actions (answered question, meeting time) are hairline rows, not boxes.

Quick access

  • Three separate actions beside the welcome, each with a one-line explanation, so Student Voice is never confused with a complaint.
  • Submit a complaint is the page's only Forest-filled element.

Your section

  • A distinct, editorial "noticeboard": the section's issues with large support numerals, who raised them (CR/GR), and the representatives' names.
  • "You support this" stays readable (fixing the low-contrast "Supported" state found in the palette preview), and "Support can't be withdrawn" is stated before acting (DEC-241).

Mobile (360px)

  • Same order: welcome, Quick access, Your actions, Your section.
  • The journey card stacks, with the reply under the track. Quick access stays as three full-width rows rather than tiles.
  • Bottom navigation with an ink active state; checked in a real 360px viewport with no horizontal overflow.

From A / B / C

  • A: sentence first, calm list.
  • B: the journey card, now conditional.
  • C: large support numbers and a section with its own identity, without C's combined launcher.

Trade-offs

  • The large greeting costs vertical space on phones; the summary sentence underneath must justify it.
  • A display serif adds a font file (see below).
  • The representatives' names give the section a human face; confirm that showing them to every student in the section is wanted.

Open questions

  • Rules for writing the summary sentence (zero, one or several updates; ordering).
  • How long a journey card stays after it is seen.
  • Meeting actions remain undefined (O29), so the meeting row only opens the meeting.

Rules in all three: the display face is used only for display moments (greetings, headline sentences, case titles, large numerals), never for buttons, forms, tables or body text. For production, the report's recommendation still applies: self-host one variable woff2 per family with font-display: swap. These previews load Google Fonts for convenience only.
