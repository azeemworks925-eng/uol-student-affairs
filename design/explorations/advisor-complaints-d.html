Batch Advisor Complaints · D: A's split pane + C's focused case

A professional workspace, not a statistics dashboard. A compact list on the left, the selected complaint as the main focus, and student, cohort and responsibility context on the right. Navigation collapses to an icon rail to give the case room. On phones: a list-first screen, then a full-screen case. Grounded in DEC-221, 222, 223, 226, 227, 205 and 283. Sample data is fictional.

Focal point

  • The selected case: the title in the display face, the journey, then the history at reading width (about 62 characters per line), because complaints can be personal and deserve careful reading.

List

  • A short headline sentence instead of tiles: "4 are waiting for your response."
  • Two groups: Waiting for you and Other open, both by date. A view, not a priority queue (DEC-226).
  • Each row: reference, flag ("Still not resolved", "New information"), title, student, cohort chip and status.
  • Search by reference ID or student name, plus two filters (cohort, status). Everything stays within assigned cohorts.

Case

  • A sticky case bar with the two case actions: Respond (Forest) and Update status. Resolving or rejecting asks for the mandatory note (DEC-221).
  • History, newest first; the student's added information never replaces the original (DEC-223).
  • The response box stays at the bottom, so replying is always one step away.

Context column

  • Student name and SAP ID, then batch, semester and section spelled out (DEC-205).
  • Responsibility: the responsible advisor and others assigned to the cohort (DEC-227), marked as a proposal because O27 is open.
  • The student's other complaints, limited to the advisor's own cohorts.

Mobile (360px)

  • Screen 1: headline, search, filters, list.
  • Screen 2: back link, case header, journey, history and response box. The context moves below the response box, while name, cohort and "Responsible: you" stay in the header line.
  • Staff use a menu button and drawer, not bottom tabs.

Below 1280px

  • The context column hides, to protect the case's reading width. A future toggle could reopen it as a drawer.

Trade-offs

  • The icon rail saves about 190px for the case but hides labels; tooltips and aria labels carry them.
  • On phones, the detailed context sits after the response box.

Open questions

  • Definition of "waiting for your response".
  • O27 (responsible advisor) and O28 (cancellation requests: a flag in the list?).
  • Whether showing the student's other complaints meets privacy expectations.
